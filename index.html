Piano Notes

Click a key, press a computer key, type a quick sequence, or write a full sheet with left and right hands. Finger numbers are worked out for you. Nothing to install, works offline.

Sheet (right and left hand)

Finger numbers: 1 = thumb to 5 = little finger, for each hand. Blue = right hand, orange = left hand. They are a sensible suggestion from note direction and span, not gospel; adjust to what your teacher says.
Load file accepts saved sheets (.json / .txt) and MusicXML (.xml / .musicxml, uncompressed). For paper sheet music: scan it with the free Audiveris app, export as uncompressed MusicXML, load it here.
Examples: C D E F G or C4 E4 G4 C5 or A3 C#4 E4. Sharps use #, flats use b (e.g. Bb4).
Computer keys (middle octave): a w s e d f t g y h u j = C C# D D# E F F# G G# A A# B
